Questions people ask

Will the library ever go behind a paywall?

No. It is free in order to be used, shared and cited — that is the whole point of writing it. Pro covers the tools built around it, not the content itself.

Is this accredited CPD?

Not currently, and it does not need to be. Self-directed reading and reflection count as CPD under the Physiotherapy Board's registration standard, provided you record them. This makes that record accurate and exportable.

Who writes it?

Content is drafted to a fixed template and then verified against its sources by a registered physiotherapist before a page is marked reviewed. Pages that have not been verified yet say so, openly, on the page.

Can I cancel?

Yes, any time, and the whole library stays free to you afterwards.
