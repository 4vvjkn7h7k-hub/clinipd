A note on what this is not

CliniPD is not an Ahpra-accredited CPD provider, and it does not need to be — under the Physiotherapy Board's registration standard, self-directed reading and reflection are legitimate CPD that you record yourself. This tool makes that record accurate and exportable. Any claim about accreditation will appear here, in writing, only once it is actually true.
